01 — ABOUT

Building an ecosystem where ideas come alive.

E-Cell, Bareilly College is a student-driven entrepreneurship and innovation community focused on creating an environment where students can explore, experiment and build.

We aim to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world entrepreneurship by encouraging students to develop ideas, acquire practical skills, connect with like-minded people and learn from the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

01

Vision

To build a vibrant ecosystem at Bareilly College where students are empowered to think innovatively, embrace entrepreneurship, and transform ideas into meaningful real-world impact.

02

Mission

To nurture entrepreneurial thinking among students by providing opportunities for learning, collaboration, mentorship, innovation and practical experience.

03

Our Impact

E-Cell aims to strengthen the entrepreneurial culture of Bareilly College by encouraging student participation, innovation, collaboration and exposure to the startup ecosystem.
